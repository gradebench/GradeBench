# **7. Product Page**

## **The GradeBench Product Suite**
Here are the products, built around a core engine and expanded through powerful modules.

### **1. Core Product: GradeBench Co-Pilot™**
**Description:** This is the heart of our platform and your flagship AI grading assistant. The GradeBench Co-Pilot™ is not a rigid algorithm; it’s a trainable partner that learns to grade exactly like you. It handles the heavy lifting of evaluating single assignments or bulk batches of handwritten answer sheets, reducing your workload by over 90%. Its core function is to become an extension of your unique expertise, scaled.
**Key Features Included:**
- **AI Single & Bulk Grading:** Seamlessly processes everything from a single student's homework to an entire batch's exam papers.
- **Authentic Annotation™:** Our proprietary feature that provides feedback directly on the answer sheet in a natural, human-like style. It maintains the personal touch students expect from their teacher, not a machine.
- **Multilingual Engine (Pragati AI):** As of September 2025, our engine supports English, Hindi, and Kannada, with Marathi and Tamil in active development to serve institutions across India.

### **2. Module: GradeBench Catalyst™**
**Description:** Go beyond grading and spark learning with GradeBench Catalyst™. This intelligent question generation tool empowers educators to create high-quality, relevant, and varied assessment materials in minutes, not hours. Whether you need multiple-choice questions for a quick quiz or complex, long-form prompts for a UPSC-style mock exam, Catalyst helps you design effective evaluations that target specific learning objectives.

**Key Features Included:**
- **Custom Question Generation:** Generate questions based on subject, topic, difficulty level, and exam format (e.g., UPSC, JEE, K-12).
- **Assessment Variety:** Create diverse test papers to prevent repetition and assess student knowledge from multiple angles.

### **3. Module: GradeBench Insights™**
**Description:** Turn raw grading data into a clear roadmap for student success. GradeBench Insights™ is our advanced analytics and reporting module that provides a comprehensive view of academic performance at every level. It eliminates guesswork, highlighting exactly where students are excelling and where intervention is needed.

**Key Features Included:**
- **Personalized Student Reports:** Every student receives a detailed, private report outlining their strengths, areas for improvement, and actionable advice.
- **Teacher & Class Dashboards:** Educators get a real-time view of their class's performance, instantly identifying common misconceptions and topics that need re-teaching.
- **Institutional Analytics:** Administrators can view high-level reports on cohort performance, curriculum effectiveness, and faculty efficiency, enabling data-driven decision-making.

### **4. Module: GradeBench Nexus™**
**Description:** The complete operating system for your educational institution. GradeBench Nexus™ integrates all our modules into a single, unified platform for seamless administration. It's the central hub where you manage classes, distribute resources, and track your entire academic operation from a single dashboard.

**Key Features Included:**
- **Class Management:** Organize student rosters, schedule classes, track attendance, and manage communications, all in one place.
- **Resource Management:** A central repository for uploading, organizing, and distributing study materials, syllabi, previous question papers, and other essential resources to your students.
